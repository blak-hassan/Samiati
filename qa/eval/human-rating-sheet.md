# Samiati Translation Quality — Human Rating Sheet

Reviewers rate each model output on three dimensions (1–5 scale):

- **Adequacy** (1–5): Does the output convey the same meaning as the source?
  5 = All meaning preserved, 1 = Meaning completely lost
- **Fluency** (1–5): Is the output natural and grammatical in the target language?
  5 = Native-like, 1 = Incomprehensible
- **Cultural appropriateness** (1–5): Is the output appropriate for Kenyan cultural context?
  5 = Fully appropriate, 1 = Culturally inappropriate
